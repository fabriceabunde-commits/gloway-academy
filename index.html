Professional certification pathway

CIFT

Certified International Food Trade Professional

An emerging competency-based programme designed for the people who make international food trade work. The credential is in development and is not yet an independently accredited or awarded certification.

01. Product & Supplier Assurance

Quality systems, traceability, packaging, labels and supplier readiness.

02. Regulatory Compliance

U.S. FDA, FSVP, prior notice, entry requirements and market-specific controls.

03. Customs & Logistics

Trade documents, freight, Incoterms, clearance and shipment risk.

04. Trade Economics

Landed cost, pricing, working capital, margins and container planning.

05. Sales & Distribution

Buyer specifications, wholesale channels, sell-through and repeat orders.
