Programme in development
Qualification overview

Built around the full trade cycle.

CIFT is designed for food manufacturers, exporters, importers, regulatory and customs teams, freight professionals, and wholesale distributors. It combines regulatory knowledge with commercial judgment and practical trade execution.

Credential governance: Professional assessment, eligibility requirements, renewal rules and independent industry oversight are proposed design features, not yet operational services.
Five competency domains
01
Food Product & Supplier Assurance

Food safety, manufacturer readiness, traceability, specifications, packaging and labeling.

02
Food Regulatory Compliance

FDA obligations, FSVP, prior notice, product-specific requirements and regulatory risk.

03
Customs, Logistics & Trade Execution

Classification, documents, Incoterms, freight, customs and shipment controls.

04
Food Trade Economics & Commercial Management

Sourcing, landed costs, pricing, profitability, financing and container planning.

05
Food Market Access, Sales & Distribution

Buyer specifications, channels, inventory performance, sales and reorders.
